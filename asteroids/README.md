# Asteroids

An original-style browser recreation of the 1979 vector arcade game, built with HTML5 Canvas and plain JavaScript.

## Play

Visit [tomlabs.live/asteroids](https://tomlabs.live/asteroids/) or open `index.html` locally.

## Controls

- Rotate: Left/Right arrows or A/D
- Thrust: Up arrow or W
- Fire: Space
- Hyperspace: H
- Pause: P or Escape
- Touch devices: on-screen arcade controls

## Arcade Details

- Inertial movement and screen wraparound
- Four-shot limit and original-style 20/50/100 asteroid scoring
- Large asteroids split into two medium, then two small targets
- Large and small flying saucers worth 200 and 1,000 points
- Increasing waves beginning with four large asteroids
- Extra ship every 10,000 points
- Risky random-location hyperspace
- Accelerating two-tone heartbeat, thrust noise, shots, explosions, and saucer audio
- Persistent local high score
